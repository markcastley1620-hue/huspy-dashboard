## Critical Fix: Sub-Community Benchmarks
- Mark flagged that comparables were using community-level benchmarks instead of sub-community — "community comparables are trash data"
- Root cause: when exact sub+bed+type combo wasn't on page 1 of Bayut scrape, code skipped to community-level
- Also: `is_sub_level` gate was blocking valid benchmarks; and communities not scraped today had zero data
- **Fixes applied:**
  1. New fallback chain: sub+bed+type → sub+bed (any type) → sub (aggregate) → STOP. Never community-level.
  2. Merges previous day's market data for communities not in today's scrape
  3. Better fuzzy matching for sub-community names
- Result: 0 community-level benchmarks. All sub-community. Sale: 134 underpriced, 132 overpriced.
- Also reverted the earlier bad change that accepted community-level benchmarks (Mark was clear: community = trash)

## Key Lesson
- Community-level market benchmarks are NOT acceptable for Huspy dashboard — ONLY sub-community level
- Mark's point: even sub-communities with count=1 in our data usually have 100-150 real listings on Bayut
- Our "count" is just what we scraped from page 1, not the real total
